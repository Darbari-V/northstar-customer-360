Customer 360: CRM Transformation & Customer Data Integration

An evolving, scenario-based case study exploring how a service business could improve lead capture, customer-data visibility, team handoffs and management reporting—starting with the business problem, not a software purchase.

Transparency: Northstar Services is fictional. This project is self-directed learning and portfolio evidence, not paid client work or a production Salesforce deployment. Scenario facts are distinguished from hypotheses; no interviews, system configuration or measured business outcomes are claimed.
01 / Why this project

The business challenge

The fictional brief describes enquiries arriving across channels, with information maintained in separate tools.

01

Fragmented information

Salespeople maintain information in spreadsheets, emails and personal notes; the brief reports fragmented customer information.

Given / reported
02

Follow-up uncertainty

Management reports inconsistent follow-ups. The causes, frequency and business impact are not yet established.

Reported concern
03

Reporting reliability

Management receives weekly Excel reports but struggles to obtain reliable sales and conversion information.

Reported concern

The BA question is not “Which CRM should we buy?” but “Which processes, information rules, ownership decisions and controls need to change—and how will we know?”

02 / Current method

Business-analysis approach

Frame business problem→Plan discovery→Model current state→Analyse gaps→Propose future state

What is known

Website enquiries reach a shared inbox; WhatsApp is handled by two salespeople; Marketing exports campaign leads to spreadsheets; Support has a ticketing tool; management receives a weekly Excel report.

Scenario facts

What requires validation

Whether enquiries are missed, why follow-ups vary, how records are duplicated, how Sales and Support exchange history, and why report figures are difficult to trust.

Open questions and hypotheses

05 / How to read this work

Evidence boundaries

Scenario fact

Information explicitly supplied in the fictional Northstar brief.

Hypothesis

Reasonable explanation or gap still requiring stakeholder/data validation.

Proposed design

A future-state idea, not an approved, tested or deployed solution.

This approach prioritises credible business-analysis reasoning and traceable documentation. It does not imply employment with Northstar, stakeholder interviews, live access to customer data, or measured improvement.
