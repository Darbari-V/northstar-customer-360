Requirements Catalogue & Traceability

A scoped, solution-neutral requirements specification for Sales enquiry visibility and ownership—connecting stakeholder needs to rules, system behaviour, data and proposed acceptance criteria.

Evidence boundary: Northstar is fictional. The author-selected rules form a coherent case-study baseline, not a stakeholder-approved business specification. No interviews, CRM deployment or execution of acceptance tests are claimed. The hypothetical change analysis and validation-question dispositions are completed documentation, not unresolved publication tasks.

Business objective & scoped need

BO-02: Strengthen lead ownership and follow-up visibility. GAP-02: Enquiry capture, ownership and follow-up. The bounded case-study need is to identify unassigned Sales work and provide appropriate visibility of enquiry ownership.

Two distinct views, two distinct rules

My Assigned Enquiries

Includes records owned by the signed-in representative regardless of status. In AC-002, Sales Rep A sees ENQ-501 and ENQ-504 (Closed), but not ENQ-502 or ENQ-503.

Selected case-study rule: AS-03 / BR-002

Active Enquiry List

Excludes records with status Closed. This does not mean that only Open records qualify. In AC-003, ENQ-501, ENQ-503 and ENQ-505 appear; ENQ-504 does not.

Selected case-study rule: AS-04 / BR-003

Completed simulated change analysis — CR-001

The original My Assigned Enquiries exercise includes Closed records. A hypothetical stakeholder later requested excluding them from that view. The analysis identified that ENQ-504 would change from included to excluded; FR-002, BR-002, AC-002 and related tests would need review.

The potential change was assessed for business rationale, view behaviour, history access, outstanding follow-ups, scope, delivery impact and traceability. It was not adopted into the v1.0 case-study baseline, and no real approval/rejection is claimed. CR-001 is complete as a documented training analysis, not an active change awaiting a decision.

Non-functional considerations & limitations

Security, performance, auditability, accessibility, privacy and retention were considered as quality categories, but no unsupported latency targets, full permission matrix or legal compliance assertions were manufactured. Later phases may establish measurable criteria with appropriate evidence.

Completion statement: The bounded Session 4 requirements specification, impact analysis and validation-question dispositions are complete for a fictional portfolio case study. Later customer-data, CRM and testing activities are separate workstreams—not missing Session 4 tasks.
