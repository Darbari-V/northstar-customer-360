# Customer 360 — CRM Transformation & Customer Data Integration

**Organisation:** Northstar Services (Fictional)  
**Project Type:** Independent IT Business Analysis Case Study  
**Status:** In Progress | Started October 2026  
**Focus:** Business Analysis · CRM · Customer Data · SaaS Implementation

## Project Overview

Customer 360 is an independently developed IT Business Analysis case study examining how a growing service organisation can improve fragmented customer information, inconsistent lead follow-ups and unreliable sales reporting.

The project follows a structured business analysis and technology transformation lifecycle, progressing from business problem investigation and process analysis to requirements engineering, data analysis, CRM solution design, integration and validation.

**Northstar Services is a fictional organisation.** This project is a simulated professional case study developed for practical learning and portfolio demonstration, not a real client engagement or completed production implementation.

**[View the published case study](https://darbari-v.github.io/northstar-customer-360/)**

## The Business Challenge

Northstar Services receives customer enquiries through multiple channels, including website forms, WhatsApp, telephone calls, referrals and marketing campaigns.

Customer and prospect information is maintained across spreadsheets, emails, personal notes and separate operational systems.

Management has reported three key challenges:

- Inconsistent lead follow-ups.
- Fragmented customer and prospect information.
- Difficulty producing reliable sales and conversion reports.

These challenges create a need to investigate existing business processes, information management practices, ownership responsibilities and reporting procedures before recommending technology changes.

## Project Objectives

The preliminary objectives are to:

- Improve consistency and completeness of customer and lead information.
- Establish clearer lead ownership and follow-up processes.
- Improve visibility and appropriate sharing of customer information.
- Support reliable and consistent sales performance reporting.
- Assess business requirements and technology options for improved customer information management.

These objectives are provisional and have not been formally approved by real stakeholders.

## Project Integrity

This case study distinguishes between:

- **Scenario facts:** Information provided in the fictional business scenario.
- **Reported problems:** Challenges described by fictional management.
- **Hypotheses:** Potential causes requiring further investigation.
- **Selected exercise assumptions:** Illustrative rules chosen to create an internally consistent case-study specification.
- **Proposed improvements:** Future-state ideas, not implemented features.
- **Completed deliverables:** Documents and diagrams actually prepared and reviewed.

No real stakeholder interviews, production deployments, executed UAT or measured business improvements are claimed.
