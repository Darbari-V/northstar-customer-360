## 1. Executive Summary

Northstar Services is a fictional growing service organisation experiencing challenges in managing customer enquiries, prospect information and sales reporting across multiple business functions.

Customer enquiries are received through several channels, while information is maintained across spreadsheets, email accounts, individual notes and separate operational systems.

Management has reported inconsistent sales follow-ups, fragmented customer information and difficulty obtaining reliable sales and conversion reporting.

This case study investigates the business situation, identifies preliminary information-management and process-improvement needs, and establishes a foundation for subsequent requirements analysis and solution evaluation.

The initial Business Analysis approach is **problem-led and technology-neutral**. No specific CRM platform or technical solution has been selected at this stage.

## 2. Business Background

Northstar Services receives enquiries through several communication channels:

- Website forms
- WhatsApp
- Telephone
- Customer referrals
- Marketing campaigns

The fictional scenario describes the following operating arrangements:

| Business Area | Current Arrangement |
|---|---|
| Website enquiries | Received through a shared email inbox |
| WhatsApp enquiries | Handled by two Sales representatives |
| Referral enquiries | Sometimes received directly by individual Sales representatives |
| Marketing campaigns | Leads exported to spreadsheets |
| Sales operations | Prospect information maintained in spreadsheets, emails and personal notes |
| Customer Support | Separate ticketing application |
| Management reporting | Weekly Excel-based sales reports |

These arrangements indicate multiple information sources and workflows. However, the existence of separate tools alone does not establish that every process is inefficient or that a particular technology is required.

Further investigation would be needed to understand actual practices, information flows, operational constraints and opportunities for improvement.

## 3. Business Problem Statement

Northstar Services does not currently have a clearly established, consistently evidenced approach for managing prospect and customer information across its different enquiry channels and business functions.

Management reports fragmented information, inconsistent follow-up activity and difficulties producing reliable sales reporting.

The differences in enquiry-handling methods and information storage may contribute to limited visibility of customer interactions, unclear ownership, incomplete records and inconsistent reporting inputs.

The precise causes, frequency and business impact of these issues remain unverified.

**The business problem is therefore defined as a need to investigate and improve the consistency, visibility and reliability of customer information management and associated operational processes.**

## 4. Symptoms, Possible Causes and Business Impact

It is important to distinguish observable or reported symptoms from possible underlying causes.

| Reported Symptom or Concern | Potential Root-Cause Hypothesis | Possible Business Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Inconsistent lead follow-ups | Follow-up responsibilities, recording practices or escalation rules may vary | Delayed responses and missed opportunities |
| Fragmented customer information | Information is distributed across separate tools and personal records | Limited customer visibility and duplicated effort |
| Difficulties with conversion reporting | Different definitions, incomplete source data or inconsistent consolidation procedures | Reduced confidence in management decisions |
| Potential duplicate customer records | Customer identities may be created independently across channels | Double counting or inaccurate customer history |
| Potential Sales-to-Support information gaps | Separate systems may lack agreed handoff or information-sharing procedures | Reduced continuity of customer service |

**Important:** Only the reported concerns and operating arrangements supplied in the fictional scenario should be treated as established inputs.

The possible root causes and consequences are analytical hypotheses requiring evidence.

## 5. Preliminary Business Need

Northstar Services needs more consistent and dependable processes and information-management capabilities to support the customer lifecycle.

Specifically, the organisation needs to establish how to:

- Capture and maintain appropriate prospect and customer information consistently.
- Identify responsibility for incoming enquiries and ongoing follow-up activities.
- Track relevant customer interactions and lead progression.
- Share customer information appropriately between authorised business functions.
- Produce accurate, consistent and timely sales performance information.

The business need describes required capabilities and outcomes rather than prescribing a specific software product.

## 8. Preliminary Scope of Investigation

### In Scope

- Enquiry sources and initial lead capture
- Lead ownership, allocation and follow-up
- Prospect and customer-information maintenance
- Existing Sales and Support information-sharing practices
- Current sales and conversion reporting processes
- Data quality, consistency and potential duplication
- Relevant technical, access-control and operational constraints
- Identification and evaluation of potential improvement opportunities

### Out of Scope at This Stage

- Selecting or purchasing a CRM platform
- Implementing Salesforce or another CRM
- Executing a production data migration
- Integrating live business applications
- Making unsupported claims about revenue or conversion improvements
- Performing an actual regulatory compliance audit

These boundaries apply to the initial business investigation and can be revisited following subsequent requirements analysis.

## 9. Initial Investigation Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Evidence Required |
|---|---|---|
| H-01 | Distributed information storage limits shared visibility | Existing record locations, access arrangements and user workflows |
| H-02 | Enquiry-handling procedures vary between channels | Channel walkthroughs, documented procedures and example records |
| H-03 | Lead ownership and follow-up rules may be insufficiently standardised | Interviews, operating procedures and assignment examples |
| H-04 | Reporting reliability may be affected by inconsistent KPI definitions or consolidation methods | Report samples, source-data definitions and reconciliation checks |
| H-05 | Duplicate or incomplete records may affect customer-data quality | Appropriate sample records and data-profiling analysis |

The hypotheses will guide subsequent investigation. They are not established findings.

## 10. Candidate Success Measures

The following measures may be useful once their definitions, feasibility and baselines have been established.

| Area | Candidate Measure |
|---|---|
| Data completeness | Percentage of enquiry records containing agreed mandatory information |
| Lead ownership | Percentage of active enquiries with an assigned responsible owner |
| Follow-up | Percentage of enquiries contacted within an agreed time threshold |
| Data quality | Duplicate-record rate and proportion of records requiring correction |
| Reporting | Reconciliation accuracy and consistency of agreed sales KPIs |
| Process effectiveness | Number of unresolved handoff exceptions or unassigned enquiries |

These represent suggested measures, not existing baseline values or achieved results.

## 11. Assumptions, Dependencies and Open Questions

The following matters require clarification during subsequent analysis:

- What constitutes a lead, opportunity, customer and support request?
- Which information fields are collected by each enquiry channel?
- What procedures currently govern assignment, reassignment and follow-up?
- Which sales KPIs are important to management?
- How are the weekly Excel reports prepared and reconciled?
- What data-quality issues actually exist?
- How is customer information shared between Sales and Customer Support?
- What privacy, security, access and retention requirements apply?
- Which existing systems could support improved processes?
- What budgetary, operational or technological constraints affect solution choices?

Resolving these questions will help distinguish actual business requirements from untested assumptions.

## 12. Recommended Next Analysis Activities

## 13. Project Integrity and Limitations

This document is based on an independent, fictional business scenario.

Northstar Services is not presented as a real client, and this artefact does not claim that actual stakeholder interviews, workshops, observations or production-system assessments have occurred.

Reported challenges, analytical hypotheses, proposed improvements and completed deliverables are distinguished throughout.

The intention is to demonstrate a structured and defensible approach to Business Analysis while documenting practical learning and project development.
