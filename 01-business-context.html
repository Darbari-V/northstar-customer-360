Business Problem & Project Context

Frame the problem without assuming that buying or configuring a particular CRM is automatically the right answer.

Independent simulation. Northstar is a fictional service organisation. These are scenario-based observations and hypotheses, not results from an actual client investigation.

Scenario facts

Northstar receives prospective-customer enquiries through website, WhatsApp, telephone, referrals and marketing campaigns. Website enquiries reach a shared Sales inbox; two salespeople handle WhatsApp; referrals can go directly to individuals; Marketing exports campaign leads to spreadsheets. Sales representatives maintain records in spreadsheets, email and notes. Support has a separate ticketing tool, and management receives a weekly Excel report.

Management reports inconsistent follow-ups, fragmented customer information and difficulty producing reliable conversion reports. Duplicate records may exist, but their incidence is unknown.

Initial business problem

Customer and prospect information is handled through different channels and storage methods. Without first understanding lead ownership, information standards, handoffs and the reporting process, Northstar cannot determine whether process, information management, or systems are primarily responsible for its reported concerns.

Business need

An agreed way to record, maintain, appropriately share and analyse lead/customer information across relevant teams, with clear responsibilities and controls.

Objectives to validate

  1. Make enquiries identifiable and traceable from receipt to disposition.
  2. Agree ownership and follow-up expectations.
  3. Provide authorised roles with appropriate customer history.
  4. Define consistent sales and conversion metrics.
  5. Investigate and manage data completeness, duplicates and consistency.

Scope and boundaries

Investigate now

Lead intake, assignment, follow-up, Sales/Support interfaces, storage practices, reporting definitions, data ownership and possible data-quality gaps.

Not claimed or implemented

No vendor chosen; no real interviews, production CRM setup, API integration, measured ROI or verified customer-data improvement.

Initial hypotheses

  • Different channel practices may produce inconsistent customer records.
  • Ownership and follow-up rules may be unclear or inconsistently followed.
  • Sales and Support may lack appropriately shared customer history.
  • Weekly reporting may suffer from data-definition or consolidation problems.

Open questions

What are enquiry volumes and lead definitions? Who assigns and reassigns ownership? How are duplicates identified? What feeds the weekly Excel report? Which information may be shared across teams, under what permissions and privacy rules?
