Artefact 006 · Proposed case-study design · CRM MVP V1

Proposed CRM Data & Solution Design

Translating Northstar's scoped enquiry-management requirements into a practical, limited CRM demonstration plan — without claiming that any Salesforce configuration has been performed.

Session 6 design prepared Design decisions still open No CRM build or UAT
Evidence boundary: Northstar Services is fictional. Artefact 006 is an independent proposed solution design based on simulated requirements and synthetic examples. No real customer, stakeholder approval, Salesforce deployment, configured validation rule, executed UAT or measured outcome is claimed.

Purpose and MVP boundary

Earlier requirements work defined a deliberately bounded scope for enquiry ownership and visibility; the data analysis examined synthetic customer–enquiry records. This design connects those findings to candidate CRM objects, fields, relationships, views, validations and future test scenarios.

Proposed MVP V1

What we plan to demonstrate

Capture synthetic Sales enquiries, maintain business-assigned owners, support scoped manager/representative views and enforce the New → Contacted ownership prerequisite.

Not part of this evidence

What has not been built

No Salesforce object configuration, integrations, API calls, reminders, dashboards, executed tests, live customer records or production environment.

Logical entities and relationships

Logical entity 1

Customer

customer_id — stable unique reference

name — customer label

phone — contact number; mandatory rule unresolved

Logical entity 2

Sales Enquiry

enquiry_id — unique reference

customer_id — proposed optional customer association

status — enquiry lifecycle value

assigned_owner_id — potentially unset business assignment

One Customer → Zero or many linked Enquiries

Each enquiry can reference at most one customer in this logical model. An optional Lookup is a candidate Salesforce relationship if Northstar explicitly adopts an identity-pending enquiry workflow. This remains an open decision (D-01), not a silently approved change to Artefact 005's candidate VAL-01.

Two different relationships: customer_id identifies the associated customer; assigned_owner_id identifies the responsible Sales representative. Missing ownership does not automatically break a valid customer link. Salesforce platform record ownership also must not be assumed equivalent to the nullable business assignment field.

Business rules mapped to proposed CRM behaviour

RequirementProposed behaviour
BR-001 / FR-001New Unassigned view shows enquiries with status = New AND business owner unset.
BR-002 / FR-002My Assigned Enquiries includes all records assigned to the signed-in representative, including Closed.
BR-003 / FR-003Active Enquiry List excludes Closed records; Open is not the only eligible non-Closed status.
FR-004Show owner identity or the display label Unassigned.
BR-004 / FR-005Reject a requested New → Contacted transition when business owner is unset.

BR-004 — business-rule pseudocode

IF current_status = "New"
   AND requested_status = "Contacted"
   AND assigned_owner_id is unset
THEN reject the requested transition;
     prompt: "Assign a Sales representative before changing
              this enquiry to Contacted."
ELSE BR-004 alone does not block the action.

Validation is not automation. Assigning a Sales representative does not automatically change an enquiry's status. A separate authorised action must request that status change. This logic is not a deployed or tested Salesforce formula.
