Stakeholder & Investigation Plan

Identify whose perspective and evidence we need before writing requirements or confirming causes.

Status: This is a proposed stakeholder map and discovery plan. No interviews, workshops, observation sessions or data reviews have yet been conducted with real Northstar employees.

Discovery questions

  1. Walk through an enquiry from arrival to first action. Who handles it?
  2. Where and how is it recorded by channel?
  3. Who assigns ownership, and how is reassignment controlled?
  4. Which fields are required and which are frequently missing?
  5. How are enquiry, lead, opportunity, customer and case defined?
  6. How are duplicate or repeat enquiries handled?
  7. What follow-up expectations exist, and how is performance evidenced?
  8. What is transferred between Sales and Support, and when?
  9. How is the weekly Excel report prepared, checked and interpreted?
  10. What access, retention, privacy and implementation constraints apply?

Triangulation and validation

Compare stakeholder descriptions against available spreadsheets, ticket histories, timestamp records, existing procedures and observation where access is permitted. Record disagreements as questions rather than silently treating one person's account as fact. The provisional As-Is model should be reviewed in a facilitated cross-functional session.

Risks to avoid

  • Leading stakeholders by asking whether Salesforce is the answer.
  • Confusing the manager's intended workflow with employees' actual workflow.
  • Assuming separate systems necessarily mean broken integration.
  • Assigning high/low influence ratings before decision rights are understood.
